Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Singh Performance ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information you provide through this website, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you complete our enquiry form, we collect the personal information you choose to give us, which may include your name, email address, phone number, the type of enquiry, and any details you share about your goals.

When you browse the site, we also collect limited technical and usage information automatically (see "Analytics and website tools" below).

Why we collect it

We use your information only to:

  • respond to your enquiry and discuss how we might work together;
  • provide and manage our coaching services; and
  • understand and improve how our website is used.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Analytics and website tools

This website uses third-party tools to help us understand how visitors use the site:

  • Google Analytics — aggregated traffic and usage statistics.
  • Microsoft Clarity — heatmaps and anonymised session recordings of general interactions (such as clicks and scrolling) to help us improve the site's design and usability. Clarity does not intentionally capture sensitive information you type.

These tools may use cookies and similar technologies and process data on our behalf. You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more detail, see Google's and Microsoft's own privacy policies.

Form submissions

Our enquiry form is delivered using Web3Forms, which transmits your submission to us by email. Your details are handled to fulfil your enquiry and are not published on the site.

How we store and protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. Information is retained only for as long as needed to respond to you and meet our legal obligations, and is then deleted or de-identified.

Accessing or removing your information

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. To do so, contact us using the enquiry form on our home page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.

If you have a concern about how your personal information has been handled, you may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.
